### Fixed

- **A wake keeps a parked fleet's crash/OOM hold, though its escalation count restarts.** A fleet parked after a crash or OOM kill rests `Asleep { until }`; the deploy wake moved it to `Waking` and threw the hold away, so the next reconcile tick spawned the crash-looping fleet through its backoff and a following request no longer failed fast. `Waking` now carries the hold, nothing spawns through it, and a deploy whose fleet is still held fails its health gate with the retry time instead. The wake (and the spawn that follows it) still resets the consecutive-crash counter to zero, so a fleet that crashes again after waking starts the backoff at 5 seconds rather than continuing the chain it had at the park; only the hold itself survives the wake.
- **A fleet generation that is on its way out no longer tears down its successor.** The crash-exit reconcile, the fail-closed `multi` spawn, the spawn error and the death before adoption cleaned their pid registration and engine proxy by fleet key alone; a stop followed by a fresh claim in that window had its proxy closed and its containers removed under it. All four now go through the documented ownership rule (`cleanup_scaffolding` with their own epoch), and only a deliberate stop forgets the fleet's OOM chain: an exit keeps the consecutive-kill backoff and the once-per-window "fleet restarted" event instead of resetting both on every restart.
