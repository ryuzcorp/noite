### Fixed

- **A wake no longer drops a parked fleet's crash/OOM backoff, and a park inside that backoff keeps it.** A fleet parked after a crash or OOM kill rests `Asleep { until }`; the deploy wake moved it to `Waking` and threw the hold away, so the next reconcile tick spawned the crash-looping fleet through its backoff and a following request no longer failed fast. `Waking` now carries the hold, nothing spawns through it, and a deploy whose fleet is still held fails its health gate with the retry time instead.
- **A fleet generation that is on its way out no longer tears down its successor.** The crash-exit reconcile, the fail-closed `multi` spawn, the spawn error and the death before adoption cleaned their pid registration and engine proxy by fleet key alone; a stop followed by a fresh claim in that window had its proxy closed and its containers removed under it. All four now go through the documented ownership rule (`cleanup_scaffolding` with their own epoch).
