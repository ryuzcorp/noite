### Fixed

- A runner restart without a container restart (dev's cargo-watch, and any in-container runner relaunch) no longer leaves the old run's fleet processes holding their ports while the new run's spawns crash-loop on `Address already in use`. At boot, before the first fleet spawn, the runner now reaps the previous run's fleet processes by this install's marker: every fleet spawn (tenant and control) carries the store-derived install tag in its process title (`argv[0]`), which stays readable in `/proc/<pid>/cmdline` even though fleet nodes are non-dumpable, and the sweep kills only processes carrying this install's marker — never another install's fleets, never a live child of the running runner — then waits (bounded) for the marker to clear and the listen ports to free. A read or kill failure only logs; the boot never fails on it. The control fleet also takes its own process group now, like a tenant fleet's, so the sweep (and any group signal) reaches the whole tree.
