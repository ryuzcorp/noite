### Changed

- The bundled RustFS 1.0.5 is now a qualified store for single-host installs: celld's own `diagnose` probe passes against the pinned image (`bucket conditional write: create, reject-create, update, reject-stale`), and the new `docker/qualify-store.ts` covers the rest of celld's storage contract — a 16-way racing conditional create with exactly one winner, read-after-write and immediate list-after-write, three ranged-read spellings, a multipart round-trip at celld's 5 MiB threshold plus abort, and delete-then-404. The script targets any S3 endpoint (`S3_ENDPOINT=... NOITE_S3_BUCKET=... bun docker/qualify-store.ts`), so operators can qualify their own store, and CI runs it against the pinned image on every PR so a RustFS bump cannot silently regress. Versioning is not required: celld fences on etags, not versions. The docs (`Storage`, `Known limits`) now say qualified instead of not-on-the-list. For multi-host production the recommendation is unchanged: your own bucket on a store celld qualifies.
