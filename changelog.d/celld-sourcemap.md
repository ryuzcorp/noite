### Changed

- Worker source maps now come from `celld deploy --sourcemap` instead of the runner's shadow esbuild bundle: the map celld writes for the app deploy is stored for the Errors tab, chained through the build's own entry map as before, so symbolicated stacks still show project-root-relative paths. The shadow bundle, its byte-verify step, and the mirrored esbuild flags, banner, and externals are deleted, so celld bumps no longer need a bundling re-verify.
- `no_bundle` apps get a Worker map again: celld collects the entry's sibling `<entry>.map` (contained in the project, symlinks refused, at most 64 MiB, v3-validated), and the runner stores it — chaining is a pass-through, the collected sources already naming the originals. A `no_bundle` entry without a sibling map celld accepts still stores no map. Requires celld `v0.6.3-ryuzcorp.7` for the collection; against an older celld those stacks render raw, as before.
