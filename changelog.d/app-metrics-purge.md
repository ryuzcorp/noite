### Fixed

- Deleting an app now deletes its telemetry with it: every metrics row keyed to the app (hourly counters, latency histograms, dimension breakdowns, the log ring, error issues/events/hours and metric versions, across all its environments) plus the ingest watermarks and compaction marks for its slug and environments. Before, those rows lingered until the retention prune — which keeps hourly rows for 720 hours and never touches some of these tables — and a reused slug or environment label could inherit the deleted fleet's watermark and "already compacted" hours. A create or rename that takes over the slug clears the stale marks before its row lands (failing the operation if the clear fails), and the purge resume pass retries whatever a crash leaves behind, keeping the tombstone until the rows are gone.
