### Fixed

- The access-log tail no longer leaves a blind spot when the backlog exceeds 8 MiB in a tick. Instead of dropping the skipped bytes blind, it stream-scans the skipped region for request hosts (64 KiB chunks, one bounded 8 KiB line prefix, never the whole file) and touches each distinct fleet's idle clock at the skip time. The burst stays uncounted — no counters, no dims — but every fleet's clock stays trustworthy through the skip, so header-inflated traffic against the unrate-limited `:80` site can no longer hold the whole fleet awake by sustaining the backlog. The sleep sweep and memory-pressure parking needed no skip gate afterward: an app that served in the burst looks active on its touched clock, and an app absent from the burst parks normally. The `access_log_skip_at` setting is gone with the gate.
