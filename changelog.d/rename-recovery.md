### Fixed

- A rename that keeps failing no longer blocks its target slug forever. The durable rename marker now records the failed automatic-resume count, the last failure class and the time; after twelve failed passes (about an hour at the loop's five-minute tick) the runner stops auto-resuming it and records a `rename stuck` app event naming both slugs. A manual retry of that target resets the count and retries, and a rename of the app back to the marker's source (_to cancel_) moves whatever the attempt had already moved back — local dirs and the `git/`, `fleets/` and `envs/` prefixes, under the AppLock and both push guards — drops the marker and frees the target, while the app keeps its slug.
- Creating or renaming onto a slug an unfinished rename reserves now answers a distinct `409` that names the way out: "the slug is reserved by an unfinished rename of another app", or for the owner's own app the source to rename back to. It no longer says "still being renamed; retry shortly".
- A slug-only rename writes only the slug-derived columns of the app row. A name-only rename runs without the deploy claim, and the move's full-row write could carry the name read before the move back over the newer one.
- A purge (delete, create's leftover wipe and a rename's target wipe) removes the `.<slug>.recreated-<ts>` salvage dirs `move_dir` leaves when a rename is retried while the reconcile loop has already recreated the old state dir. The match is the exact `.<slug>.recreated-` prefix, so a neighbouring slug (`ab` vs `abc`) is never touched.
