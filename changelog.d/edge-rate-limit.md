### Fixed

- **A configured edge rate limit is now a real ceiling.** The limiter budgeted `ceil(rpm/6)` requests per 10 s window, so 1/min admitted six requests a minute, 7/min twelve, and 100/min 102. The budget is derived from the window and rounded down (`floor(rpm × 10 s / 60 s)`), and below 6/min the window widens to an exact divisor of a minute, so 1/min is one request per 60 s and 5/min one per 12 s.
- **`Retry-After` reports the real remaining time instead of a stamp that has already freed.** It is the time until enough of the key's stamps leave the window for it to admit again (rounded up to whole seconds, never below 1), so after a lowered rate it no longer reports the oldest stamp's expiry while several still block the request, and a client that honours the header is admitted instead of looping on `429`s.
- **A request refused by the app ceiling no longer spends the client's budget.** The client zone and the app zone are decided in one step and both record a stamp only when both admit the request, so a burst against a saturated app does not throttle the visitor a second time.
- **Eviction and sweep no longer clear a live throttle.** A zone at its 65536-key cap evicted the least-recently-seen quarter, so a client could spray fresh keys to flush its own (or another client's) throttle and carry on; a table push with a short idle dropped the same state. Eviction now only drops keys whose budget has recovered, a sweep keeps every key still inside its window, and a full zone with nothing evictable refuses new keys for up to one window instead of resetting a limit.
